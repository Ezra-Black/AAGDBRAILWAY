Refunds

Last updated September 16, 2026

Short version: we do not offer refunds on membership charges or custom angel-name graphics once payment has gone through. These are made-to-order digital pieces, not a returnable object.

What that covers

  • The $10 monthly membership billed by Stripe
  • Graphics requested under that membership
  • Any older archive-shop purchase already paid

Changing your mind, not liking the finished style, picking the wrong graphic, or a typo in an angel name after we have begun are not refund reasons. Read the preview and the names on your profile carefully before you subscribe or send a request. Cancel membership anytime in the billing portal — that stops future charges; it does not refund the current period.

The only exceptions

Contact us and we will make it right if:

  • Stripe charged you twice for the same order
  • You paid and we never delivered the file, and we cannot complete it

In those cases we may refund the duplicate or failed charge, or remake the piece. We do not refund because a chargeback is easier than writing us — please message the studio first.

Membership graphics

Graphics are included with membership. If a request cannot be made, we can decline or close it. That is not a membership refund.

How to reach us

Contact page (signed-in inbox) or aaggraphics@protonmail.com. Include the email used for membership and the angel name so we can find the request.
